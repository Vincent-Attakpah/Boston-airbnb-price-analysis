Model 2: log(price) regressed on property characteristics, control variables and neighborhood, fitted to all 3,418 listings with complete data. Percent differences are 100(eb − 1). Dollar differences apply these percentages to a typical listing: an entire home for 3 guests with 1 bedroom, 1 private bathroom, a 2-night minimum and 37 amenities, which the model prices at $391 per night in Downtown.

A neighborhood is significantly different from Downtown when its 95% confidence interval excludes zero. Leather District (3 listings) and Longwood Medical Area (17) have very wide intervals. Beacon Hill's discount partly reflects one host who operates many of its listings.
